Consider this system of three simultaneous equations: x plus y plus z equals 6, 2x plus 3y plus z equals 11, and x plus 2y plus 3z equals 14.

Solving by trial and error or repeated substitution quickly becomes tangled.

Is there a systematic method to solve all three equations efficiently?

The answer is Gaussian Elimination.
